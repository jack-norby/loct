Mental

Thought · Task

Your attention is the most fought-over thing you own. This month, decide where it goes before an app decides for you.

Thought Task
31hrs

back this month if you cut one hour of screen time a day

Renew Your Mind.

Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect. Romans 12:2

Thought

Guard what goes in.

Whatever you feed your mind shapes what you think about when nobody’s watching. Be picky about what gets in.

Set a screen-time limit

Pick a daily limit for social apps and let your phone enforce it.

Journal for 5 minutes

What happened, what you’re thinking about, what you’re thankful for.

Learn something for fun

A podcast, a documentary or a new skill. Something you chose.

Fill the quiet moments

Waiting in line or walking to class? Try a sermon, a Scripture app or worship instead of a feed.

Pro tips

Delete, don’t hide.

Take the apps off your phone for October. You can reinstall them November 1st if you still want them.

Try grayscale.

Turn your phone’s screen gray. Apps are a lot less tempting without the color.

Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence, if there is anything worthy of praise, think about these things.Philippians 4:8

Guard your input.

Your mind is being trained every day by

What you watch.What you scroll.What you listen to.What you reach for when you’re tired.

Task

Make your tools work for you.

Classes, work, friends and church all want a piece of your week. A little planning keeps the important things from getting buried.

Plan tomorrow tonight

Five minutes before bed: what’s due, where you need to be and your top three tasks.

One calendar for everything

Classes, deadlines, shifts and plans, all in one place you actually check.

Notifications off

Do Not Disturb on while you study. Check messages on breaks.

Start early

Do the first 20 minutes of a big assignment the day you get it.

Hardest thing first

Tackle the task you’re dreading while your energy is highest.

Pro tips

Plan tomorrow tonight.

Write tomorrow’s top three tasks on paper before bed. Wake up already knowing.

Phone in another room.

Studies keep finding that just having your phone nearby drains focus, even face down.

Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, making the best use of the time, because the days are evil.Ephesians 5:15–16
