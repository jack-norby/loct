Your attention is the most fought-over thing you own. This month, decide where it goes before an app decides for you.

Renew Your Mind.

Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect. Romans 12:2

Thought

Create more than you consume.

When you’re tired, your brain reaches for the easiest hit, and that’s usually a feed. Locktober flips it. When you feel like scrolling, make something instead.

Tired? Create.

When you want to consume media, write, draw, play guitar or cook something.

No short-form content

No Reels, TikTok or Shorts for the whole month.

Read 5 pages a day

A fun book, not for class. Five pages is small enough to never skip.

What do I actually enjoy?

Write down what you love doing and thinking about. Do more of it.

Pro tips

Delete, don’t hide.

Take the apps off your phone for October. You can reinstall them November 1st if you still want them.

Keep a “make” list.

Write five things you could create in 15 minutes. Pull from it when the urge hits.

Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence, if there is anything worthy of praise, think about these things.Philippians 4:8

Create > Consume

Your mind is being trained every day by

What you watch.What you scroll.What you listen to.What you reach for when you’re tired.

Task

Make your tools work for you.

Texts, the calendar and homework time all compete for the same brain. Go analog where you can, block what distracts you and work in timed chunks.

Paper & analog everything

A paper planner, a notebook and a to-do list you can hold.

Content blockers on everything

Phone, laptop and browser. Make the bad option annoying.

Timers

Work 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. Use the timer on this page.

Show up, fully present

Go to every class and be fully immersed in the lecture. Notes only, no tabs.

AI is a tool, not a cheat code

Let it explain things and quiz you. Don’t let it do the work for you.

Texts, calendar, homework

Answer texts in batches, live by the calendar and block real homework time.

Pro tips

Plan tomorrow tonight.

Write tomorrow’s top three tasks on paper before bed. Wake up already knowing.

Phone in another room.

Studies keep finding that just having your phone nearby drains focus, even face down.

Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, making the best use of the time, because the days are evil.Ephesians 5:15–16
