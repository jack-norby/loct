Locktober · Heart

Emotional

Beauty · Belonging

You can sleep eight hours and still feel empty. Your heart gets refilled by beauty you actually notice and people who actually know you.

Beauty Belonging

Look up. Show up.

Consider the Lilies.

Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin, yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these. Matthew 6:28–29

Beauty

Look for what God made.

God filled the world with beauty and then gave you eyes to see it. Most days we walk right past it staring at a screen. Slow down long enough to notice.

One good thing

Write down one good thing from your day before bed.

Make something

Draw, cook, play an instrument, take photos. Beauty you help make counts.

Listen on purpose

Play one album start to finish with nothing else going on.

Go somewhere new

A park, a trail or a coffee shop you’ve never been to.

Pro tips

Put the phone away.

You don’t have to photograph everything. Some things are better just looked at.

October is showing off.

The leaves won’t look like this again until next year. Go see them.

The heavens declare the glory of God, and the sky above proclaims his handiwork.Psalm 19:1

Today’s beauty prompt

Find the most colorful tree on your walk to class.

Belonging

You weren’t made to lock in alone.

Belonging doesn’t happen by accident. It happens when you keep showing up for the same people, whether that’s productive time, intentional time or just fun.

Share a meal

Eat with someone instead of alone, at least once a day.

Reach out

Call or text someone you haven’t talked to in a while, like family back home.

Show up to community

Church, a small group, a club or a team. Keep showing up for the same people.

Phones down with people

When you’re with people, be with them. Phones away at meals and hangouts.

Pro tips

Be the inviter.

Don’t wait to be included. Text the group chat first: “Dinner at 6?”

Mix all three.

Study together (productive), ask a real question (intentional), then go do something dumb (fun).

And let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together, as is the habit of some, but encouraging one another.Hebrews 10:24–25
