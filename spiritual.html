This is the lock that holds the rest together. Real rest is a person, and he’s inviting you to come to him.

“Come to me… and I will give you rest.”

Matthew 11:28

Rest for Your Soul.

You keep him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on you, because he trusts in you. Isaiah 26:3

Locktober pairs with The Spiritual Workout Plan. Read, memorize, accountability and truth all count here. Find this week’s reading and verse on the home page.

Purpose

Obey today.

You don’t have to figure out your whole life this month. Purpose usually looks like doing the next right thing God already told you to do, and doing it again tomorrow.

Daily obedience

Do the thing you know God is asking of you today, even the small things.

Integrity

Be the same person in private, in class and at church. That includes how you use AI for school.

Serve someone

One unasked-for act of kindness a day.

Pro tips

Ask one question.

Every morning: “Jesus, what’s one thing you want me to do today?” Then write it down.

Integrity check.

Ask your lock-in partner the hard one every week: “Have you been completely honest?”

He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the LORD require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?Micah 6:8

The next right thing.

Whoever walks in integrity walks securely, but he who makes his ways crooked will be found out.Proverbs 10:9

Peace

Start with God, not your phone.

Whatever you look at first sets the tone for the whole day. Give the first few minutes to God before the notifications get a turn.

Prayer closet

The first couple minutes of the day go to God, before the phone.

Worship music

Put it on during the walk to class, the drive or the gym.

Breathe & pray

When stress builds, stop for one breath prayer. Try the one on this page.

Sabbath Sunday

No tracking, no workout, little or no school. Fast until after church.

Pro tips

Find a closet.

Pick one spot: a chair, a corner or your car. Go there every morning so the habit has a home.

Make Sunday different.

Finish homework Saturday night. After church, reflect on the sermon and the catechism, then plan for community group.

But when you pray, go into your room and shut the door and pray to your Father who is in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will reward you.Matthew 6:6
