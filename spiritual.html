Purpose · Peace

This is the lock that holds the rest together. Real rest is a person, and he’s inviting you to come to him.

Purpose Peace

“Come to me… and I will give you rest.”

Matthew 11:28

Rest for Your Soul.

You keep him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on you, because he trusts in you. Isaiah 26:3

Locktober pairs with The Spiritual Workout Plan. Read, memorize, accountability and truth all count here. Find this week’s reading and verse on the home page.

Purpose

Live for something bigger.

You don’t have to figure out your whole life this month. Purpose usually starts small: loving the people in front of you and using what God gave you to serve them.

Serve someone

One act of kindness a day that nobody asked for.

Pray for others

Pray for one person by name every day, and tell them you did.

Encourage someone

Send a text or say something that builds someone up.

Use your gifts

Volunteer at church or in your community once a week.

Pro tips

Ask one question.

Every morning: “Jesus, what’s one thing you want me to do today?” Then write it down.

Look around.

Notice who’s sitting alone in class or at church. Go sit with them.

He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the LORD require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?Micah 6:8

Made on purpose.

For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them.Ephesians 2:10

Peace

Be still.

Peace doesn’t come from a quieter schedule. It comes from time with the one who holds it. Make room for him every day.

Read the Bible daily

Follow the Spiritual Workout Plan reading for the day.

Pray for 5 minutes

Any time of day. Talk to God about what’s actually going on.

Memorize the verse

Say this week’s memory verse out loud a few times.

5 minutes of quiet

No music, no phone. Sit with God in the quiet.

Pro tips

Same time, same place.

Tie it to something you already do, like right after lunch or before bed. Habits stick when they have a home.

Pray the Psalms.

Don’t know what to say? Open to a psalm and pray it back to God in your own words.

But when you pray, go into your room and shut the door and pray to your Father who is in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will reward you.Matthew 6:6
