Get Locked In.

Rest isn’t doing nothing. It’s refilling the energy you spend every day in four places: your body, your mind, your heart and your soul.

Locktober started as two friends writing goals on a whiteboard and turned into a whole college ministry thing. The idea is simple: pick a few goals in each area, check in every day and have someone ask you how it’s going.

It’s not about pace or perfection. Small steps of faithfulness add up over 31 days.

The Morning Routine.

The same order every day, so you don’t have to think about it. Here’s a simple one to start from. Make it yours.

  1. Wake upSame time every day. Feet on the floor before you check anything.
  2. WaterA full glass before the coffee.
  3. Time with GodToday’s Bible reading and a few minutes of prayer. The reading is below.
  4. MoveShower, stretch or take a quick walk outside for morning light.
  5. BreakfastProtein and real food. Don’t skip it.
  6. Plan the dayWrite your top three tasks on paper. Then head out.

Track it as one goal: check it off when you hit every step.

Lock-In Partner

“Two are better than one… For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow.” Ecclesiastes 4:9–10

Find a friend, a roommate or your small group and go through these once a week.

  1. PhysicalDid you sleep, move, eat and get outside the way you planned?
  2. MentalWhere did your attention go? Did you create more than you consumed?
  3. EmotionalWhat beauty did you notice? Who did you spend real time with?
  4. SpiritualHow was your time with God this week? Where did you see him at work?
  5. Into the lightWhat sin or temptation do you need to bring into the light?
  6. Integrity checkHave you been completely honest?

Pro tips

Go through the questions twice. First round, give a quick one or two sentence answer to each. Then go back to anything that needs more time. That’s where the real growth happens.
