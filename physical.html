Locktober · Body

Physical

Sleep · Sweat · Snack · Sunlight

Your body isn’t separate from your walk with God. When you’re running on four hours of sleep and gas station snacks, everything else gets harder.

Sleep Sweat Snack Sunlight

“Glorify God in your body.”

1 Corinthians 6:20

Train the Temple.

Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body. 1 Corinthians 6:19–20

Sleep

Guard your 7–9.

Sleep is the first rest God built into every single day. Protect it like it matters, because it does.

7–9 hours a night

Not less than 7. Not more than 9.

Same wake-up, every day

Pick a time and keep it, weekends included. Your body loves a rhythm.

Screens off before bed

The last 30 minutes of the day are phone-free. Read, pray or talk instead.

Set up the cave

Blackout curtains or a sleep mask, a cool room, and earplugs if your roommate snores.

Pro tips

Count backwards.

Need to be up at 7? Lights out by 11 at the latest. Set a bedtime alarm, not just a wake-up one.

Phone sleeps elsewhere.

Charge it across the room. Get an actual alarm clock if you have to.

It is in vain that you rise up early and go late to rest, eating the bread of anxious toil; for he gives to his beloved sleep.Psalm 127:2
7–9

hours a night, with the same wake-up every day

Sweat

Move it or lose it.

Your body has two enemies here: atrophy, from not moving at all, and injury, from doing too much too fast. Aim for steady, not heroic. Pick a plan you can actually keep for 31 days.

Train 3–5× a week

Lift, run, play a sport or take a class. Pick what you’ll actually show up for.

Move every day

On non-workout days, walk. Around 7,000 steps a day is a solid target.

Warm up and stretch

Ten minutes before and after keeps you training instead of injured.

One full rest day

Take a real day off each week. Recovery is part of training.

Pro tips

Put it on the calendar.

Schedule workouts like a class. If it’s not on the calendar, it’s just a nice idea.

Bring a friend.

A gym partner turns “maybe tomorrow” into “I’m outside your dorm.”

Warm up. Seriously.

Five minutes of warm-up beats five weeks of a tweaked back. Injury is the fastest way to lose your Locktober streak.

For while bodily training is of some value, godliness is of value in every way, as it holds promise for the present life and also for the life to come.1 Timothy 4:8

Snack

Steady energy. No crash.

Sugar gives you a spike and then drops you on the floor right before your 2pm class. Whole foods keep the line flat and steady. Got allergies or a specific diet? Swap in your own goals on the home page.

Mostly whole foods

Food that looks like it came from a farm, not a factory. Less added sugar.

Snack smart

Nuts, fruit, jerky, yogurt or cheese. Protein and fiber keep you steady.

Water bottle, all day

Refill it between classes. Aim for pale yellow, not dark.

Skip fast food

Cook, hit the dining hall or pack something from home.

Pro tips

Pack it the night before.

Decide when you’re full, not when you’re starving in line at the C-store.

Drink water first.

Half the time “hungry” is actually “thirsty.” Carry a bottle.

So, whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do all to the glory of God.1 Corinthians 10:31

Sunlight

Bookend the day outside.

Morning light tells your body it’s time to be awake, and that helps you fall asleep at night. Watching a sunrise or sunset also reminds you who made the sky.

Watch the sunrise & sunset

Catch at least one of them every day.

Morning light

Ten minutes outside within half an hour of waking up, even if it’s cloudy.

Walk outside

Take the long way to class. Leave the earbuds out sometimes.

Pro tips

Bundle up.

October mornings get cold. Grab a hoodie and a coffee and go anyway.

Make it a hangout.

Sunset on a roof or a field with your roommates counts for Belonging too.

The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness.Lamentations 3:22–23
